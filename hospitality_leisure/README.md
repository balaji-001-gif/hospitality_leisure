# HospitaLeisure ERP

Complete Hospitality and Leisure ERP Solution built on Frappe Framework v15 and ERPNext v15.

## Modules
- Hotel Management
- Restaurant Management
- Spa & Wellness
- Gym & Fitness
- Events & Banquets
- Finance & Revenue
- Attraction Management
- Entertainment Management
- Campsite Management
- Marina Management
- Cruise Operations
- Sports Management

## Installation
1. Get the app: `bench get-app https://github.com/balaji-001-gif/hospitality_leisure.git`
2. Install on site: `bench --site yoursite.com install-app hospitality_leisure`
3. Migrate: `bench --site yoursite.com migrate`
